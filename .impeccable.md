## Design Context

### Users
People building or exploring a small social network as a learning project. They need a clear, calm product UI that makes auth, posting, liking, following, and profile navigation easy to understand.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, human. The app should feel like a thoughtful notebook for short updates, not a loud consumer platform.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light interface with tinted off-white surfaces, charcoal text, a single desaturated green accent, sharp-but-soft 8px radii, and restrained motion. Avoid purple/blue AI gradients, generic card grids, neon glows, emoji, and stock-social clutter.

### Design Principles
- Keep the product usable before decorative.
- Make empty and error states specific and useful.
- Use asymmetry through spacing and hierarchy, not visual chaos.
- Prefer borders, rhythm, and type contrast over heavy shadows.
- Every control must have hover, active, focus, loading, and disabled states where applicable.
